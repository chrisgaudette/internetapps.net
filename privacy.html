Privacy notice

Effective date: October 1, 2026

This notice explains how Internet Apps handles information in connection with this informational website and inquiries sent to internetapps.net@gmail.com.

Contact form and email inquiries

The contact form prepares a message in your default email application. It does not submit the form contents to an Internet Apps server or store them in a website database. Preparing the message does not send it; you choose whether to send it from your email application.

If you send an inquiry, Internet Apps receives the information you include, such as your name, email address, company, role, and description of your project. We use this information to respond, discuss a potential engagement, and manage the resulting correspondence. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information in an initial inquiry.

Service providers and technical information

Email correspondence is processed through email providers, including Gmail for the contact address above. Website hosting and delivery providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details, and requested pages to deliver and protect the site. Their processing is also subject to their own applicable policies.

Cookies and analytics

The current website code does not set its own cookies or load an analytics service. Hosting or delivery services may separately process technical information. If we introduce analytics or change how we use information, we will update this notice.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information. Processing an inquiry involves the email and hosting providers described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

This website does not specify a fixed retention period for inquiry correspondence. Information you send may remain in email correspondence and related business records after the conversation ends.

Questions and deletion requests

To ask about information you sent, request a correction, or request deletion, email internetapps.net@gmail.com. We will review your request and explain any information that needs to be retained for an ongoing engagement or legal obligations. Please do not send identity documents unless we specifically arrange an appropriate way to verify a request.

Changes to this notice

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
